Jinsong Liu

Postdoctoral Associate, Weill Cornell Medicine, Cornell University

I am an operations research/reinforcement learning researcher working on agentic reasoning and tool-use, with a foundation in RL theory and large-scale optimization. My work bridges these methods into healthcare AI (at Weill Cornell Medicine) and high-performance optimization systems, with a track record of impactful open-source releases including a state-of-the-art deep-research agent (1,500+ GitHub stars) and the industry’s first GPU-accelerated LP solver (200+ stars).

I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate at Weill Cornell Medicine, Cornell University, where my research focuses on AI for Healthcare. Before joining Weill Cornell, I was a visiting PhD student at the SC Johnson College of Business, Cornell University, working on LLM agents for decision making. I received my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics in 2025. Before that, I received my Bachelor’s degree from Tianjin University in 2020.

Research Interests:

  • Agentic RL and autonomous decision-making
  • Reasoning and tool-use in LLM agents
  • Operations Research, optimization and RL algorithms and theory
  • AI/OR for healthcare
  • Mathematical solver development
