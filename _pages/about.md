I am a reinforcement learning researcher working on agentic reasoning and tool-use, with a foundation in RL theory and large-scale optimization. My work bridges these methods into healthcare AI (at Weill Cornell Medicine) and high-performance optimization systems, with a track record of impactful open-source releases including a state-of-the-art deep-research agent (1,500+ GitHub stars) and the industry's first GPU-accelerated LP solver (200+ stars).

I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate at [Weill Cornell Medicine](https://weill.cornell.edu/), Cornell University, where my research focuses on AI for Healthcare. Before joining Weill Cornell, I was a visiting PhD student at the [SC Johnson College of Business](https://business.cornell.edu/), Cornell University, working on LLM agents for decision making. I received my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from [Shanghai University of Finance and Economics](https://english.sufe.edu.cn/) in 2025.

**Research Interests:**
- Agentic RL and autonomous decision-making
- Reasoning and tool-use in LLM agents
- Reward modeling and preference learning
- RL algorithms and optimization theory
- AI for healthcare
