ABIP is an implementation of an ADMM-based interior-point method for linear and conic optimization problems. It combines the strengths of first-order methods (ADMM) with interior-point approaches to solve large-scale optimization problems efficiently.

**Repository:** [github.com/leavesgrp/ABIP](https://github.com/leavesgrp/ABIP)
